# CLSLR-6 preregistration

CLSLR-6 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At canonical heights divisible by
36 it measures six completed-minute BTC confirmation-interval returns and the
weights of blocks H+1 through H+6. Returns 4–6 must share one strict sign, and
the aggregate weight of blocks 4–6 must strictly exceed blocks 1–3. No fitted
threshold, prior rank, duration ordering, reversal, or completion condition is
used.

After H+6 and the conservative two-hour header-time embargo, the policy follows
the unanimous late sign from the next five-minute open for six elapsed hours at
fixed 0.5 gross. Controls cannot be promoted. RV20 q90 remains a later audit
and every gate is terminal.
