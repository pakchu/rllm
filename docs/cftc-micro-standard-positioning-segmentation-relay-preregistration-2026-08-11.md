# CFTCMSPS-168 preregistration

`CFTCMSPS-168` compares weekly open-interest-normalized positioning changes across the standard CME Bitcoin contract and the Micro Bitcoin contract. It follows standard-contract asset-manager positioning relative to Micro-contract leveraged-money positioning only in an elevated realized-variation state.

This is a futures-only cross-contract participant-segmentation mechanism. It does not reuse the failed standard-contract option clock, its events, or a diagnostic control. Candidate incidence, Gross9 rows, execution prices, and outcomes remain sealed.
