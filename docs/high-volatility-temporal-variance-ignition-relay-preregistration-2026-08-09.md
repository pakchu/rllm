# HVTVIR-12 preregistration

HVTVIR-12 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At each 02:00 UTC decision it uses
288 completed five-minute returns. Completed 24-hour realized variation must
rank in its causal upper 35%, and the share of quadratic variation contributed
by the final three hours must rank in its causal upper 20%. The first 21-hour
return and final three-hour return must have one strict sign.

The policy follows that common sign from 02:05 UTC for twelve elapsed hours at
fixed 0.5 gross. This is within-day temporal variance ignition, not SPVTA's
spot/perpetual variance transfer, realized skew, return-volume covariance, or
high-low/body variance. No prior event set or diagnostic control is reused.

RV20 q90 remains a post-stage audit only. Source support, Gross9 novelty, and
strict sequential economics are terminal at first failure; no window,
variance-share formula, rank, direction agreement, side, hold, clock, subset,
or control may be repaired or promoted.
