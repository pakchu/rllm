# HVSENC-8 preregistration

HVSENC-8 freezes one continuous Sample Entropy candidate before source
incidence. It measures the conditional probability that two similar normalized
two-return templates remain similar after one more return. A fresh lower-tail
entropy state is eligible only when completed eight-hour BTC variation is in
its causal upper 35%; the completed displacement is followed for eight hours.

This is not sign entropy, ordinal permutation entropy, Lempel–Ziv parsing, or
recurrence-plot determinism. The template dimension, Chebyshev tolerance,
normalization, histories, ranks, onset, side, clock and hold are immutable.
No candidate incidence, Gross9 row, execution price, post-entry return, funding
PnL or RV20 value was opened before this freeze, and controls cannot be promoted.
