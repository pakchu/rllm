# APWSR-12 preregistration

APWSR-12 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or outcomes are opened. It subtracts the standardized 00:00-08:00
UTC USDHKD move from the standardized USDKRW move, thereby removing common
dollar and currency-board pressure. A strict-prior absolute spread rank of at
least 0.70 and completed BTC variation rank of at least 0.65 trigger an 08:05
UTC entry opposite relative won stress for twelve hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

Prior HPPRR, MXRBR, and EMDF evidence is known, but APWSR reuses none of their
events or controls. Its relative peg-adjusted spread is neither the raw HKD or
MXN move nor the failed common EM dollar factor. No pair, spread, rank, side,
session, clock, hold, or subset may change after incidence is opened.
