# BHARR-48 preregistration

BHARR-48 is frozen before retarget headers, source incidence, Gross9 clocks,
BTC execution prices, funding, or outcomes are opened. Bitcoin mainnet block
headers are the authority; Blockstream and mempool.space are independent
transports whose height hashes, header bytes, nBits, and H+6 confirmation
objects must agree exactly.

At every positive height divisible by 2016, BHARR computes the change in log
difficulty from the prior epoch and subtracts the preceding epoch's change.
Positive acceleration maps long and negative acceleration maps short. The
event becomes available only at block H+6, enters at the first strictly later
five-minute open, requires prior-24h BTC variation rank at least 0.35, and
holds 48 elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross. Source support, Gross9 novelty, and
strict economics are sequential terminal gates with no repair.
