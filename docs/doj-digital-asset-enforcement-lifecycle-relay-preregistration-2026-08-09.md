# DDAELR-24 preregistration

DDAELR-24 is frozen before DOJ press-release incidence, Gross9 clocks, BTC
execution prices, funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened.  Its sole event
authority is the official DOJ News API.  Raw paginated responses, UUIDs,
publication dates, URLs, and update metadata must be preserved.

A fixed digital-asset taxonomy identifies relevant releases.  An unambiguous
charge, indictment, arrest, complaint, or new civil action is an initiation
and maps short.  An unambiguous conviction, sentence, guilty plea, seizure,
forfeiture, recovery, compensation, disruption, or takedown is a resolution
and maps long.  Both/neither classifications are ineligible; conflicting
eligible sides on one publication date invalidate that date.  Date-only
records become available conservatively at 22:00 UTC and enter at 22:05 UTC.

The event is active only when the completed prior-24-hour BTC realized
variation ranks at least 0.65 against strictly prior eligible publication-day
observations.  It holds 24 elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross.  Source support,
Gross9 novelty, and strict economics are sequential first-failure gates.  No
source, keyword, class, timing, volatility, direction, hold, or subset repair
is permitted.
