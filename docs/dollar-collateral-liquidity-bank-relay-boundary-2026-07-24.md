# DCLB-864 candidate boundary — dollar-collateral liquidity/bank relay

## Selection

Select one new source-seen candidate:
**DCLB-864 — Dollar-Collateral Liquidity/Bank Relay**.

DCLB will relate three independently timestamped official U.S. dollar
liquidity surfaces:

1. the Federal Reserve H.4.1 consolidated balance-sheet liquidity stock;
2. the New York Fed's daily overnight reverse-repo operation; and
3. the Federal Reserve H.8 commercial-bank balance-sheet release.

The provisional object is the causal ordering and agreement/disagreement among
a central-bank stock, a daily reserve-drain/release flow, and a weekly bank
funding/liquidity state. A deterministic composer will own opportunity timing
and LONG/SHORT direction. A later RLLM may only execute that fixed side or
abstain from compact relation and transition tokens.

The `864` suffix reserves a 72-hour consequence horizon. This file does not
freeze the exact normalizers, relation algebra, side, availability join,
entry, controls, support gates, or economic evaluator. Those must be committed
before any DCLB joint-state incidence or post-entry BTC outcome is computed.

## Why this follows the CXRT failure

CXRT had dense source support but failed before comparators and outcomes. Its
equal-vote CBOE state developed a 30-event same-side selection run, the option
surface reproduced the composite side about 87% of the time, and the tail
surface supplied RELIEF on only 6.91% of selection events.

DCLB changes the observable rather than repairing those gates:

- it removes CBOE term, tail, and aggregate option-flow inputs;
- it moves from same-date equal voting to asynchronous public-release
  relations;
- it separates a liquidity stock, an executed facility flow, and a banking
  transmission state; and
- its longer horizon matches the slower public-balance-sheet mechanism rather
  than CXRT's next-session risk-sentiment clock.

No CXRT threshold, vote, side, run cap, reproduction cap, or 24-hour hold is
relaxed or reused.

## Prior-family contamination and no-repair limits

This source family has substantial prior outcome exposure:

- FLCC H.4.1 component-concordance variants failed risk-adjusted and temporal
  stability gates;
- ORFR overnight-RRP flow release was strong in 2021 and negative in 2022;
- H8DM's standalone bank-stress tail failed 2020–2022;
- BDRC's exact H.8/SOFR sign concordance failed source support; and
- later federal-liquidity and repo candidates exposed additional aggregate
  diagnostics.

DCLB is therefore not a pristine global-history discovery and may not:

- reuse FLCC's selected 4/8-release tails or component-breadth rule;
- promote ORFR's primary or one-day-delta control;
- reuse H8DM's selected absolute-stress tail;
- replace BDRC's SOFR input with RRP while retaining exact sign conjunction;
- select an H.4.1, RRP, or H.8 horizon from BTC outcomes;
- add a BTC price, funding, volatility, or calendar regime gate after seeing a
  weak year; or
- claim that an accounting release directly causes BTC demand.

The new falsifiable object must be the **asynchronous relation and transition
among the three public dollar-liquidity clocks**. A single source may be a
control or context token but cannot be silently promoted after a failed
composite.
