# HVPIFSR-8 preregistration

HVPIFSR-8 freezes one outcome-blind BTC candidate before opening its source
incidence. At each actual funding settlement it compares the realized funding
rate with a deterministic one-minute replay proxy derived from the completed
time-weighted premium-index path and the published interest-plus-clamp map.
Only a fresh upper-tail absolute surprise during elevated trailing BTC
variation is eligible; the trade fades the surprise for eight elapsed hours.

The one-minute implied value is explicitly a replay proxy rather than a claim
of bit-exact reconstruction of Binance's internal sampling implementation.
No candidate clock, Gross9 row, execution price, post-entry return, funding PnL,
or RV20 value was opened before this freeze. The source, novelty and sequential
economic gates are immutable, and diagnostics cannot be promoted.
