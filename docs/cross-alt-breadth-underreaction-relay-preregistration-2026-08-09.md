# CABUR-8 preregistration

CABUR-8 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or outcomes are opened. At 04:00, 12:00, and 20:00 UTC it requires a
four-of-six alt return majority, an upper-30% median absolute alt impulse, BTC
absolute return below that median impulse, and upper-35% completed prior-24h
BTC variation. It follows the alt majority from decision plus five minutes for
eight hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

CABUR measures magnitude underreaction. It does not reuse CABLR's opposite-
sign events, CABER's confirmation fade, or a flow-leadership control. No
universe, block, breadth, impulse, underreaction, rank, side, clock, hold, or
subset may change after incidence is opened.
