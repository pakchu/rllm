# MFDH-8 preregistration

MFDH-8 is frozen as one singleton before its source incidence, Gross9 clocks,
execution prices, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At each actual BTCUSDT
funding settlement, it requires three consecutive same-signed nonzero funding
rates with strictly increasing absolute magnitude. The completed 16-hour BTC
return must oppose that funding sign and have a strictly-prior 270-settlement
midrank of at least 0.60 after 180 valid observations. It follows the price
direction from the exact settlement-plus-five-minute open for eight elapsed
hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

The mechanism represents increasingly expensive leveraged inventory that is
already losing against price, followed by a deleveraging handoff. It is not a
repair of STCR-72 or HVKAR-12 and uses neither their event clocks nor their
features. Other design ideas reviewed before this freeze are not fallback
candidates after any MFDH failure.

RV20 q90 is a later stress slice, never an entry filter. Only after every
full-calendar economic stage passes may MFDH be decomposed against the
identical-clock, identical-gross forced-long comparator on the full calendar
and causal RV20 q90 observations.

Source support, every Gross9 novelty comparison, and strict economics are
terminal first-failure gates. No funding path, rank, history length, side,
hold, RV20 definition, threshold, subset, diagnostic control, comparator, or
failed gate may be changed or rerun.
