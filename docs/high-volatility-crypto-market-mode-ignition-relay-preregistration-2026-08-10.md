# HVCMMI-8 preregistration

HVCMMI-8 is frozen before source incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding or outcomes are opened.  At each exact eight-hour boundary it forms a
six-alt minute-return correlation matrix over the completed prior eight hours.
An event occurs only when the first eigenvalue share newly enters its causal
upper quartile while completed BTC variation ranks in the upper 35%.  The
positively oriented first loading projects the final alt hour; its strict sign
is followed in BTC from the next five-minute open for eight elapsed hours at
fixed 0.5 gross.

This return-market-mode object is distinct from BTC-versus-alt median
correlation fracture and from price-free aggressive-flow PCA.  It reuses no
event set or diagnostic control.  Every gate is terminal at first failure;
the universe, eigensystem, thresholds, side, clock, hold and subset cannot be
repaired after incidence is opened, and RV20 q90 remains a later audit only.
