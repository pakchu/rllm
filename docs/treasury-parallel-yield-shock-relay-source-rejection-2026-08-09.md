# TPYSR-24 terminal source-support rejection

TPYSR-24 was rejected before Gross9 rows, execution prices, or post-entry
returns were opened.

The frozen official Treasury 2-year/10-year source and completed pre-entry BTC
source produced 14/20/21/13 train/test/eval/final events. Every stage met its
minimum event count and minority-side requirement. Test and eval also met the
monthly dispersion requirement. Train's largest month contained 7 of 14 events
(`0.5000`), however, and final's largest month contained 6 of 13 events
(`0.4615`). Both exceed the preregistered maximum monthly share of `0.45`.

Changing either rank threshold, the 24-hour hold, the D+1 availability clock,
the tenor pair, or a stage subset would alter the frozen candidate. Diagnostic
controls cannot be promoted. TPYSR-24 is therefore terminally rejected
unchanged; Gross9 novelty and economics remain unopened.
