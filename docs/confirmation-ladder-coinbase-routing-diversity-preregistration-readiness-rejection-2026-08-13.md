# CLCBRD-6 preregistration readiness — terminal rejection

## Decision

Reject CLCBRD-6 before source incidence, market rows, Gross9 clocks, execution
prices, funding, future returns, or PnL are opened. The committed preregistration
is not amended and no source evaluator or collector is authorized.

## Failure

The proposed raw first-coinbase-output script fingerprint is an immutable object
not used by BCRT or the existing size, weight, transaction-count, fee, endpoint,
UTXO, witness, and difficulty candidates. The retired Mempool summary transport
also explicitly allowed a separately frozen homogeneous transaction-level panel
as future work. Those facts are not enough to make the committed protocol
execution-ready.

1. The preregistration names public Blockstream Esplora as the historical
   primary transport and checks only every 257th selected block against Mempool.
   It does not prove a complete-window archive, frozen schema, stable historical
   availability, or full fail-closed transport parity. The prior source decision
   required an owned Bitcoin Core node or a separately justified complete
   transport; neither exists in this environment.
2. A raw first-output script fingerprint proves script reuse or turnover only.
   It does not identify a miner, pool, owner, or economic entity. The claim's
   language about broadened reward routing and heterogeneous block production
   exceeds the observable.
3. The clock, late-three return unanimity, side, hold, and controls reuse the
   established confirmation-ladder architecture. Although the source object is
   distinct, that increases the burden on source completeness and explicit
   ladder-family novelty rather than curing the missing transport proof.

## Boundary

The adversarial review used only committed definitions and source-governance
documents. No transaction endpoint was called, no script fingerprint or event
incidence was computed, and no new market or outcome row was read. A collector
draft written after preregistration but before any execution was deleted rather
than used to repair the frozen contract.

CLCBRD-6 is terminal. Future work may not change its identity, wording,
transport, parity sample, fingerprint, diversity relation, clock, side, hold, or
gates. A genuinely new candidate requires an independently complete source
contract and cannot inherit this candidate's unexecuted collector design.
