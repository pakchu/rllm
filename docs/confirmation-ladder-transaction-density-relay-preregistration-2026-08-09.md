CLTDR-6 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At canonical heights divisible by
36 it measures six completed-minute BTC confirmation returns and canonical
block transaction density. Returns 4–6 must share one strict sign, while
aggregate transactions per unit weight in blocks 4–6 must strictly exceed
blocks 1–3. This composition feature is scale-normalized and uses no fitted
threshold or prior rank.

After H+6 and a two-hour header-time embargo, the policy follows the unanimous
late sign from the next five-minute open for six elapsed hours at fixed 0.5
gross. Controls cannot be promoted. RV20 q90 remains a later audit and every
gate is terminal.
