# HVQPLR-6 preregistration

`HVQPLR-6` is frozen before source incidence, Gross9 rows, execution prices, or outcomes are opened. At fixed eight-hour boundaries it measures which of three Binance spot BTC stablecoin-quote books most strongly predicts the next-minute common return of the other two books. It follows a uniquely concentrated leader for six hours only when its late direction agrees with the common block displacement and BTC variation is causally elevated.

This is price-leadership concentration, not stablecoin taker-flow consensus, contemporaneous quote disagreement, or spot/perpetual basis correction. First failure is terminal and all controls are diagnostic only.
