# DUTF-6 preregistration

DUTF-6 is frozen before candidate incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices, funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. A canonical non-empty Bitcoin block is an event only when its header timestamp follows its predecessor by at least 1,800 seconds and its own weight is at most 1,000,000 units. The event waits for six confirmations and then uses the prefix maximum header timestamp and mediantime plus a two-hour embargo, rounded up to a five-minute boundary.

From the next five-minute open, the unchanged policy fades the strict completed two-hour BTC impulse for six elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross. Events are processed by increasing height and globally reserved without replacement.

The mechanism is failed backlog clearing despite ample template time, not a generic interval, fee, throughput, empty-template, retarget, hashrate, candle-shape, volume, flow, funding, premium, or OI rule. RV20 q90 is not an entry filter and remains sealed until all four strict economic stages pass. Every gate is terminal at first failure; diagnostic controls cannot be promoted.
