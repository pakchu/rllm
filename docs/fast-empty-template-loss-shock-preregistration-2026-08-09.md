# FETLS-6 preregistration

FETLS-6 is frozen before candidate incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. A canonical coinbase-only Bitcoin
block is an event only when its header timestamp is at most 120 seconds after a
predecessor weighing at least 3,200,000 units. The event waits for six
confirmations and then uses the prefix maximum header timestamp and mediantime
plus a two-hour embargo, rounded up to a five-minute boundary.

From the next five-minute open, the unchanged policy fades the strict completed
two-hour BTC impulse for six elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross. Events are
processed by increasing height and globally reserved without replacement.

The mechanism is a discrete fast empty-template failure after a heavily
occupied block, not a generic interval, fee, throughput, backlog, retarget,
hashrate, candle-shape, volume, flow, funding, premium, or OI rule. RV20 q90 is
not an entry filter and remains sealed until all four strict economic stages
pass. Every gate is terminal at first failure; diagnostic controls cannot be
promoted.
