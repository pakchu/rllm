# CMMTCR-24 preregistration

CMMTCR-24 is frozen before incidence, Gross9 clocks, prices, funding, or
outcomes are opened. At 02:00 UTC it requires completed BTC 6h, BTC 24h, and
ETH 6h returns to share one strict sign while BTC prior-24h variation ranks in
the upper 35%. It enters at 02:05 UTC in that common direction for 24 hours at
fixed 0.5 gross.

This is not STCR's BTC-only 3d/14d twice-weekly clock or an ETH lead-lag
residual. No event set or control is reused, and no market, horizon,
concordance, volatility, side, clock, hold, or subset may change after
incidence is opened.
