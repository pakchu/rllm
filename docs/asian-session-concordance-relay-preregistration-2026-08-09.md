# ASCR-16 preregistration

ASCR-16 is frozen before incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices, funding,
or post-entry outcomes are opened. At daily 08:00 UTC it reads the exact
00:00-04:00 and 04:00-08:00 completed one-minute paths. If both four-hour
returns have the same strict sign, it follows that direction from 08:05 for
sixteen elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

This is an intraday session-concordance and midnight-handoff mechanism. It is
not completed-day decisive-close momentum, Monday/weekend liquidity handoff,
multi-day trend concordance, run persistence, flow, funding, or a fitted
volatility filter. RV20 q90 remains sealed as a later stress audit only.

Source, novelty, and economic gates are terminal at first failure. No session,
concordance, side, entry, hold, subset, or diagnostic control may be repaired
or promoted.
