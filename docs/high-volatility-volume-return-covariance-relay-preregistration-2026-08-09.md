# HVVRCR-12 preregistration

HVVRCR-12 is frozen before incidence, Gross9 clocks, prices, funding, or
outcomes are opened. At 02:00 UTC it correlates 288 completed five-minute
intrabar returns with log quote volume. Upper-30% absolute correlation and
upper-35% realized variation trigger a 02:05 UTC position in the correlation
direction for twelve hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

This continuous full-day covariance is not VSPCR's volume-quartile
disagreement, a dominant-volume bar, or close-to-VWAP displacement. No prior
events or controls are reused, and no formula, rank, side, clock, hold, or
subset may change after incidence is opened.
