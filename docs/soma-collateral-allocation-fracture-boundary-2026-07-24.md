# SCAF-48 candidate boundary — SOMA collateral-allocation fracture

## Selection

Select one source-seen, outcome-unseen candidate:
**SCAF-48 — SOMA Collateral-Allocation Fracture**.

SCAF will use the cross-sectional allocation geometry inside daily New York Fed
SOMA securities-lending auctions. The provisional economic object is a causal
change in the mismatch among:

1. submitted borrowing demand across individual CUSIPs;
2. lendable SOMA inventory across those CUSIPs;
3. accepted awards relative to submitted demand; and
4. the cross-sectional dispersion of accepted lending fees.

The candidate is not an aggregate liquidity-level rule. It asks whether
Treasury-collateral demand is becoming concentrated, rationed, and
security-specific, or whether substitution across securities is broadening.
A deterministic composer will own opportunity timing and direction. A later
RLLM may only execute that fixed side or abstain from compact relation tokens.

The `48` suffix reserves a provisional 48-hour consequence horizon. This file
does not freeze the exact distributions, divergence statistic, tie handling,
causal batch rule, transition algebra, side, hold, controls, support gates, or
economic evaluator. Those must be committed before any SCAF feature or
candidate incidence is decoded. Forty-eight hours is selected prospectively as
two complete daily-auction transmission cycles and deliberately differs from
the 72-hour SLCS and DCLB holds; no SCAF market outcome has been opened.

## Why this follows the DCLB failure

DCLB-864 was retired before comparators and outcomes. Its exact intersection
of H.4.1, overnight RRP, and H.8 produced:

- a 196-day maximum train entry gap against a 60-day cap;
- no 2023 Q1 event;
- train RRP-only same-side reproduction of 85.71%; and
- selection stale-RRP reproduction of 85.71%.

SCAF changes both the observable and state geometry:

- it removes H.4.1, overnight RRP, and H.8;
- it uses daily CUSIP-level auction distributions rather than a sparse
  intersection of weekly and daily release states;
- it represents allocation mismatch and substitution topology rather than
  aggregate dollar-liquidity direction; and
- it permits several weak cross-sectional relations to compose without
  requiring an exact three-source sign conjunction.

No DCLB normalizer, transition, side, gap threshold, reproduction threshold, or
72-hour policy clock is relaxed or reused.
