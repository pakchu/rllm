# HVTICC-8 preregistration

HVTICC-8 freezes one aggressive-flow concentration candidate before source
incidence. Each completed eight-hour block is divided into 96 exact five-minute
bins. The signal is the signed HHI of dollar taker imbalance across those bins;
its sign identifies the concentrated aggressive side and its magnitude measures
whether directional urgency is localized rather than diffuse.

This is neither HVTIFP's lag-one flow dependence nor HVTISI's same-slot
aggregate-flow innovation. Formula, ranks, onset, side, offset clock and hold are
immutable. Incidence, Gross9, prices, returns, funding PnL and RV20 remained
unopened at freeze.
