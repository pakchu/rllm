# EMDFR-12 preregistration

EMDFR-12 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. Each weekday it measures the
13:00-16:00 UTC canonical-dollar return in USDMXN, USDINR, and USDCNY,
standardizes each pair against strictly prior valid sessions, and takes their
median. A strict-prior absolute-factor rank of at least 0.70 and completed
24-hour BTC variation rank of at least 0.65 trigger a 16:05 UTC entry opposite
the factor sign for twelve elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

The outcomes of DFSR, MXRBR, and SHRR are known, but their exact events are not
reused. EMDFR freezes a different three-country emerging-market source set and
an earlier session close; it is neither a threshold repair nor a promoted
control. No source, factor, rank, direction, clock, hold, or subset may change
after incidence is opened. RV20 q90 remains a post-stage audit and every gate
is terminal.
