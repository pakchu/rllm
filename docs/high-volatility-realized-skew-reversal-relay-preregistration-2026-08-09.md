# HVRSR-12 preregistration

HVRSR-12 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At each 02:00 UTC decision it uses
288 completed five-minute returns to calculate realized variation and the
normalized signed third moment. Strict-prior daily ranks must place variation
in the upper 35% and absolute realized skew in the upper 30%. The candidate
enters at 02:05 UTC opposite the skew sign for twelve elapsed hours at fixed
0.5 gross.

This is a third-moment exhaustion mechanism, not the previously tested
positive-versus-negative semivariance mass, sign entropy, or bipower jump
variation. Those outcomes are known but no event set, threshold, clock, or
diagnostic control is reused. No moment formula, rank, direction, clock, hold,
or subset may change after incidence is opened. RV20 q90 remains a post-stage
audit and every gate is terminal.
