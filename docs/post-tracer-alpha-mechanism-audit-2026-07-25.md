# Post-TRACER alpha mechanism audit — 2026-07-25

## Outcome

Select one new candidate for an outcome-blind source-support freeze:

**LAMB-21 — Liquidity-Aware Microstructure Braid, twenty-one-line
seven-day-context eight-hour target-position RLLM.**

LAMB combines two slow public dollar-liquidity releases with two fast,
completed Binance aggregate-trade views:

1. Federal Reserve H.4.1 net-liquidity change;
2. New York Fed overnight-RRP amount and participation change;
3. exact BTC quantity-lattice cohort relations; and
4. same-millisecond BTC cascade relations.

This audit did not decode a new joint LAMB state, timestamp incidence, future
return, funding cash flow, reward, model output, trade, PnL, CAGR, or MDD.
Selection used repository schemas, source audits, frozen manifests, builders,
and already documented predecessor failures.

## What TRACER changed

TRACER-4H retired before outcomes because its 2023 source support failed,
primarily during a sustained leadership-validity outage. That failure rules
out another candidate whose identity depends on all dense exchange surfaces
being simultaneously valid at every short boundary.

The successor therefore needs:

- an asynchronous public-release state carried only within a declared
  freshness interval;
- explicit age and invalidity rather than silent forward fill;
- a slower action clock that reduces five-minute noise and turnover;
- target-position transitions instead of a sparse fixed-side event rule;
- primitive relational tokens rather than a previously failed scalar score;
- a model role centered on conditional temporal deduction; and
- deterministic ownership of all numeric transforms, clocks, execution,
  costs, rewards, and drawdown.

## Why LAMB is not a repair

LAMB does not reuse the retired policies:

- no QLCD score threshold, fixed side, 288-bar hold, or selected side flip;
- no SMCC q99.5 event threshold, rare-event claim, 144-bar hold, or event
  count cap;
- no BAFR q90 event clock, two-hour reversal side, or failed score;
- no DCLB H.8 anchor, exact three-release intersection, deterministic macro
  side, or 864-bar fixed event;
- no TRACER four-hour three-exchange-surface validity conjunction; and
- no IVPLH intrinsic-volume event anchor or fixed handoff side.

The quantity-lattice and same-millisecond files are used only as causal
primitive containers. LAMB aggregates every valid completed five-minute row
into fixed eight-hour states and exposes categorical relations to a
target-position policy. It does not search a new threshold around a failed
event clock.

LAMB **does reuse the outcome-blind QLCD source cohort definitions**: coarse is
an exact multiple of 100 mBTC, medium is a multiple of 10 but not 100 mBTC,
and fine is every remaining 1 mBTC increment. That reuse is contamination, not
a hidden novelty claim. The definitions, transform bytes, and coarse/fine
mapping are frozen before LAMB incidence and may not be changed after support
or outcomes. QLCD's failed standalone result remains negative evidence. A
later LAMB evaluator must include `no_lattice` and lattice-only killer
baselines; LAMB fails if the cross-domain braid does not add value beyond that
known weak primitive family.

## Falsifiable thesis

The weak signals are not expected to be profitable alone.

The thesis is that identical fast order-flow pressure has different meaning
under different slow dollar-liquidity transitions:

- supportive H.4.1 and RRP transitions can sponsor persistent fast pressure;
- restrictive transitions can make the same pressure fragile or reversible;
- disagreement between exact-size cohorts and concentrated millisecond
  cascades can distinguish broad participation from local mechanical impact;
  and
- the ordered evolution of these relations can matter more than any current
  scalar value.

An RLLM is justified only for that finite relational deduction. It is not
asked to estimate arithmetic, read raw prices, forecast a numeric return, or
override source validity.

## Contamination boundary

This is **not** a globally pristine alpha discovery.

- H.4.1 and ON RRP predecessor aggregate outcomes are known.
- QLCD 2020–2022 failed economically and its 2023+ stages stayed sealed.
- SMCC opened source incidence but no market outcomes.
- Many BTC calendar outcomes have been viewed elsewhere in the repository.

The claim available to LAMB is narrower:

> the exact four-source asynchronous composition, twenty-one-line symbolic
> sequence window, and rolling target-position policy have not been evaluated.

Consequently:

- 2020–2022 are development/history years;
- 2023 can only be described as candidate-specific transfer, not a globally
  pristine holdout;
- historical transfer evidence requires source extension and
  candidate-specifically sealed 2024, 2025, and 2026-YTD schedules under one
  frozen evaluator;
- those schedules are transaction-style historical holdouts, not real-time
  prospective observations, because the calendar outcomes already exist;
- a live profitability claim still requires a forward shadow/live interval;
  and
- no weak year may repair a token, clock, rank horizon, action space, model,
  reward, or gate.

## Stop condition

LAMB retires unchanged at the first failed source, coverage, diversity,
stability, control, replay, or forbidden-access gate. Only a complete
source-support pass may authorize a separate economic/RLLM evaluator freeze.
