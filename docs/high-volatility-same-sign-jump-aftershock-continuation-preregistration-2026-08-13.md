measures the fraction of consecutive jump arrivals separated by at most three
five-minute bars. A fresh upper-tail excitation state during elevated realized
variation follows the signed mass of all completed jumps for eight hours.

absolute returns. Scale, jump multiple, minimum count, gap, ranks, onset, side,
clock and hold are immutable. Candidate incidence, Gross9 rows, execution
