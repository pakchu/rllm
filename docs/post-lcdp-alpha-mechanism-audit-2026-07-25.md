## Why CEFS-D1 is not CSPG, CXRT, OPRR, CVTR, CTHD, or CIHM

The Cboe family is not pristine. Earlier outcomes and source-support results
are negative evidence that must remain visible:

- CVTR used a scalar term-rotation rule and failed sealed Stage 1.
- CTHD used a thresholded tail-hedge disagreement and failed sealed Stage 1.
- CIHM used a thresholded option-flow migration score and failed sealed Stage
  1.
- CXRT used an equal-vote majority side and failed source relational
  composition.
- OPRR required a sparse rank-rotation conjunction and failed source support.
- CSPG used fixed global ordinal buckets and failed token stationarity before
  outcomes.

CEFS-D1 therefore may not use:

- a source-owned long or short side;
- a scalar score or thresholded event clock;
- an equal-vote majority;
- a hand-coded cross-surface consensus, stress leader, relief leader, or
  pressure aggregation;
- an absolute, expanding, or rolling percentile rank;
- any CSPG token boundary or signature;
- any CVTR, CTHD, CIHM, CXRT, or OPRR trigger;
- a selected subset of Cboe dates; or
- a rule chosen from already opened Cboe returns.

Every exact common source date must become an action-independent decision
state after sequence warm-up. The permitted representation is limited to
primitive exact relation edges within one surface, whether each edge persists
or flips from its immediately prior common state, a short ordered history of
those unaggregated edges, and current target position. The deterministic
composer may not translate them into a source-owned market side or
cross-surface vote. A separate mechanism commit must freeze the formulas,
vocabulary, availability clock, sequence length, source-support gates,
controls, and failure action before any CEFS source value is decoded.

## Falsifiable thesis

Each Cboe surface is a weak and previously unsuccessful standalone signal.
The candidate thesis is narrower:

> BTC response may depend on the ordered transition topology between U.S.
> option term structure, tail-risk pricing, and aggregate option participation,
> rather than on an absolute level, extreme, vote, or one-day scalar score.

An RLLM is admissible only to rank `TARGET_LONG`, `TARGET_FLAT`, and
`TARGET_SHORT` from a finite categorical sequence. Deterministic code owns
source validation, arithmetic, chronology, execution, costs, funding, reward,
drawdown, and all safety-flat decisions. Raw numbers, dates, ranks, returns,
and hidden reasoning are forbidden model inputs.

## Earliest stop gate

CEFS-D1 retires before outcomes if any of the following occurs:

1. the three frozen panels or manifests fail exact schema/hash validation;
2. the exact common-date chronology cannot reproduce the documented causal
   next-session clock;
3. the percentile-free token language collapses into a dominant or
   nonstationary vocabulary under prospectively frozen source-only limits;
4. stale, source-permuted, or time-reversed controls do not materially change
   the language;
5. future append changes any prior state;
6. the state schedule reproduces a retired Cboe trigger rather than a dense
   action-independent policy; or
7. any market, funding, outcome, reward, model, or post-2023 source value is
   accessed before the source-support pass.

Only a complete source-support pass may authorize a separately committed
economic/RLLM evaluator.
