# HVTBCR-24 preregistration

HVTBCR-24 is frozen before candidate incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution
prices, funding or outcomes are opened.  It measures the daily change in the
official U.S. Treasury `2*5y-2y-10y` butterfly.  A rising five-year belly
relative to the two- and ten-year wings maps short BTC; a falling belly maps
long BTC.  The signal is eligible only when causally completed seven-day BTC
variation ranks in the upper 35%, and enters the next day at 12:05 UTC for 24
elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross.

This is a curvature-factor experiment, not the prior same-sign two-/ten-year
parallel shock or the opposite-sign two-/ten-year twist.  It introduces the
official five-year yield, reuses no prior event set or control, and cannot be
repaired by changing maturities, side, threshold, clock, hold or subset after
source incidence is opened.  Every gate is terminal at first failure; RV20 q90
remains a later report-only audit after all four economic stages pass.
