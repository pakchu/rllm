# FARR-6 terminal source-contract rejection

FARR-6 was rejected before candidate incidence, Gross9 rows, execution prices,
or post-entry returns were opened.

The preregistration required a unique BTCUSDT funding row at each 00:00 and
08:00 UTC boundary. The frozen query found 2,616 target boundaries and 184
boundaries with two rows, all between 2026-01-18 and 2026-06-29. Selecting a
preferred duplicate by timestamp, value, or insertion order was not frozen and
would alter the final-period source contract.

The candidate is therefore terminally rejected unchanged. No deduplication,
clock repair, novelty evaluation, or economic stage is authorized.
