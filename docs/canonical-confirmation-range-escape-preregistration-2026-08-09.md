# CCRE-6 preregistration

CCRE-6 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. Canonical heights divisible by 36
are confirmed through H+6 and embargoed for two hours beyond the prefix maximum
header/median time. At the resulting five-minute boundary D, the completed
signal bar close must strictly exceed the high or fall below the low of the 288
exact five-minute bars immediately preceding that signal bar.

The policy enters at D+5m and follows the escape side for six elapsed hours at
fixed 0.5 gross. It uses no block-return path, payload, fitted threshold, rank,
or RV20 entry filter. Controls cannot be promoted; every gate is terminal.
