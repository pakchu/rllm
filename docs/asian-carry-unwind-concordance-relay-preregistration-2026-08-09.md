# ACUCR-12 preregistration

ACUCR-12 is frozen before incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices, funding,
or outcomes are opened. It requires completed 00:00-08:00 UTC USDAUD and
USDJPY moves to agree as either AUD weakness plus JPY strength (risk unwind)
or the exact symmetric risk relief. Upper-35% completed BTC variation then
triggers an 08:05 UTC entry with relief and against unwind for twelve hours at
fixed 0.5 gross.

This is raw two-leg Asian carry concordance, not SHRR's standardized three-leg
European magnitude tail, USDJPY alone, or a common-dollar factor. No events or
controls are reused, and no pair, concordance, side, clock, hold, or subset may
change after incidence is opened.
