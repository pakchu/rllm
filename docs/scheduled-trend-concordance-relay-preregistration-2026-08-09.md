# STCR-72 preregistration

STCR-72 is frozen before source incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At exact Monday and Thursday 00:00
UTC decisions, it follows the common nonzero sign of completed 3-day and
14-day BTC returns. Entry is 00:05 UTC, hold is 72 elapsed hours, and gross is
fixed at 0.5.

This is a full-calendar policy. The exact causal RV20 rolling-q90 state is a
separate volatility stress slice, not an entry filter. Only after every
sequential full-calendar stage passes may the frozen policy be decomposed
against an identical-clock, identical-gross forced-long comparator on both the
full calendar and RV20 q90 observations. The q90 candidate return and residual
candidate-specific alpha must both be positive.

Source support, every Gross9 novelty comparison, and strict economics are
terminal first-failure gates. No schedule, horizon, agreement, direction,
hold, RV20 definition, threshold, subset, comparator, or failed gate may be
changed or rerun.
