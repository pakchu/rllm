Absolute endpoint migration must newly enter its strict-prior q90 tail, at least four of five share increments must agree with the endpoint direction, and cumulative spot aggressive flow must have that same strict sign.

After six confirmations and a conservative prefix-maximum header-time plus two-hour embargo, the unchanged policy follows the migration side from the next five-minute open for six elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross. This ordered confirmation-ladder path is not a fixed-window spot/perp ratio, ticket-size rule, price return, VWAP, funding, premium, OI, block throughput, or prior diagnostic.
