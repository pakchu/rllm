# OSPLR-24 preregistration

OSPLR-24 is frozen before OFAC action incidence, BTC execution prices, Gross9,
funding, or outcomes. Dated official Recent Actions and SDN change archives are
the only authorities; the mutable current SDN list is forbidden as history.

Unambiguous designation, sanctions, or restrictive SDN-update actions map
short. Explicit removals and general-license publications map long. Both or
neither classes are inactive, same-side actions on one date deduplicate, and
opposite sides invalidate the date. Date-only actions become available at a
conservative 22:00 UTC and enter at 22:05 UTC for 24 hours. Prior-24-hour BTC
variation must rank at least 0.65 against strictly prior valid OFAC action
days. Source, novelty, and strict economics are terminal first-failure gates
with no taxonomy, timing, direction, hold, or subset repair.
