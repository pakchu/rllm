# TPCT-120 candidate boundary — tri-party composition topology

## Selection

Select one source-support-seen, outcome-unseen policy axis:

**TPCT-120 — Tri-Party Composition Topology Policy**.

TPCT observes the internal composition of private tri-party repo reported by
the Office of Financial Research (`TRIV1`, explicitly excluding Federal
Reserve transactions). It represents several individually weak relations among
tenor shares, collateral shares, and their volume-weighted rate geometry.
One compact policy chooses exactly one action:

```text
LONG
SHORT
ABSTAIN
```

The provisional causal chain is:

1. accept only a complete preliminary-vintage `TRIV1` source vector;
2. delay the vector by the already frozen conservative OFR availability clock;
3. compare its composition and rate geometry only with strictly prior source
   vectors;
4. emit a compact categorical grammar of levels, disagreements, leaders, and
   transitions;
5. let one policy rank long, short, and abstain without a source-owned side;
6. reserve every eligible opportunity before the policy action is known; and
7. enter only after one complete five-minute latency bar and exit after exactly
   120 elapsed hours.

The `120` suffix reserves a five-day consequence horizon, one complete
business-week transmission cycle. Exact arithmetic, source completeness,
relations, token grammar, prior history, simultaneous-release handling,
reservation, split-boundary accounting, support floors, reward, baselines,
model, preference/RL method, controls, costs, and qualification gates must be
frozen in later committed units before any TPCT-derived value, token,
opportunity, label, market outcome, or post-2023 source row is opened.

This boundary is not an alpha result. It selects one falsifiable dense-state
policy experiment.

## Why this is not an OFR candidate repair

The OFR preliminary source and four prior candidate incidences are already
source-seen. None opened BTC prices, funding, future returns, PnL, CAGR, or MDD.
Their exact predictive objects are nevertheless retired:

- `RVFC-72` used simultaneous cross-venue dispersion/concentration and
  collateral disagreement. Its sparse consensus clock failed support and may
  not be relaxed into a component policy.
- `RMSR-72` used a first-passage race between cross-venue collateral-mix and
  collateral-rate extreme states. Its terminal balance and maximum-gap rules
  failed and may not be repaired.
- `RCRE-72` used the signed product of GCF-minus-TRIV1 agency-share and rate
  gaps. Its required quadrant geometry failed and may not be reinterpreted as
  a favored quadrant.
- `DMSH-168` used one DVP venue, outstanding-versus-transaction maturity
  composition, and a delayed rate-confirmation handoff. Its sparse,
  concentrated, one-sided clock failed and may not be widened.

TPCT changes the predictive object:

- it uses only the private tri-party `TRIV1` segment, never DVP, GCF, or `TRI`;
- it models the full simultaneous **within-segment composition topology**
  rather than a cross-venue spread, signed venue product, or ordered
  confirmation event;
- every valid delayed source vector is an opportunity state rather than only a
  threshold transition;
- no individual relation owns a long or short side;
- no conjunction, precursor, terminal, or source extreme is required for
  eligibility;
- abstention is a policy action and cannot release or move the globally
  reserved opportunity; and
- the hold is prospectively fixed at 120 hours rather than reusing the failed
  72-hour or 168-hour event identity.

TPCT is explicitly a **source-support-seen successor**. Repeated OFR
source-support attempts informed the choice to test a dense grammar rather
than another sparse intersection. This weakens any claim that TPCT's source
geometry was selected independently of prior incidence, even though no OFR
market outcome has been opened. Every later result must retain that
disclosure, use the frozen within-candidate familywise correction, and require
an unchanged post-2023 source extension before production promotion.

TPCT may not reuse a prior candidate's threshold, vote, transition, side,
precursor, terminal, product, support exception, or failed control as a hidden
gate. Prior OFR source values and incidences must be disclosed in every later
artifact. The exact TPCT token rows and all TPCT market outcomes remain
unopened.

## Why this is an RLLM-shaped task

The policy is not asked to forecast a raw price or extrapolate one numeric
series. It must reason over compositions such as:

- whether overnight/open activity is gaining share while term-rate pressure is
  rising or falling;
- whether maturity concentration agrees with or diverges from collateral
  concentration;
- whether Treasury, agency/GSE, corporate-debt, and other-collateral activity
  rotate together or substitute for one another;
- whether volume leadership agrees with rate-pressure leadership;
- whether the current topology persists, rotates, or reverses relative to the
  immediately prior valid state.

No single relation is claimed to be strong. The hypothesis is that the joint,
causal symbolic grammar changes the conditional utility of long, short, and
abstain.

TPCT remains one policy model. There is no analyzer/trader pair, free-form
chain of thought, model-generated feature, or model-controlled accounting.
Source validation, exact-rational composition, strict-prior ranks,
scheduling, costs, reward, risk metrics, and execution remain deterministic
code.

Current sleeve position, executability, portfolio conflicts, and prior TPCT
actions are deterministic accounting/execution guards only. They are not
policy tokens or model inputs.

A language model is justified only after frozen cheap causal baselines
demonstrate transferable pre-2023 structure. The RLLM must then add value over
those baselines without using 2023 for prompt, token, hyperparameter,
checkpoint, reward, threshold, or control selection.

## Causal availability boundary

Every required row remains subject to the source-audit clock:

```text
available_at_utc = max(
    observation_date 00:00 UTC + 8 elapsed calendar days,
    2020-09-10 00:00 UTC,
)
```

The mechanism must freeze exact vector-completeness and rejection rules before
decoding values. The source-only parser may then decode only the sixteen
required values to apply those rules before emitting any primitive, rank,
token, or opportunity. Nulls, disclosure edits, missing required rows,
nonpositive denominators, nonfinite values, duplicate rows, and semantic
overlap invalidate the vector. No fill, interpolation, zero substitution,
final-vintage repair, or neighboring bucket substitution is allowed.

Rows sharing one availability timestamp form one causal batch. No member may
enter another member's strict-prior reference. Every row in the batch computes
its rank only from complete rows whose availability is strictly earlier than
the batch. Only the greatest complete observation date in a batch may become a
decision row; earlier batch rows are history only. Every current decision
token, transition, source-age relation, and reservation is finalized against
states from strictly earlier availability batches. Only afterward do complete
current-batch rows enter future histories in ascending observation-date order.
This tie-break is source-value- and token-blind.

The provisional execution clock is:

1. complete the vector and its strict-prior categorical state;
2. round causal availability upward to a five-minute boundary;
3. consume one additional complete five-minute bar;
4. enter only at the following five-minute open;
5. reserve opportunities globally in chronological order before action;
6. hold exactly 120 elapsed hours / 1,440 five-minute bars; and
7. require entry and exit to remain inside one declared half-open split
   `[split_start, split_end)`, with `entry >= split_start` and the exit-open
   timestamp strictly `< split_end`.

An `ABSTAIN`, policy failure, or unexecutable market row does not release the
reservation for a later state. Suppressed opportunities are never queued.

## Rejected alternatives for this turn

### BCRT gap repair — closed

BCRT-72 passed every token-support check but failed its frozen development
maximum-gap rule because deterministic year-boundary split containment created
five-day gaps. That result is retired unchanged. TPCT's boundary-aware gap
definition is prospective for a new source and cannot reinterpret BCRT.

### OFR Federal Reserve residual — not selected

Although `TRI` and `TRIV1` have inclusion/exclusion semantics, their difference
must not be labeled ON RRP, and residual weighted-rate inference introduces
denominator and revision fragility. TPCT uses the directly published private
tri-party panel instead.

### Another sparse OFR transition — closed

Lowering a prior threshold, dropping an unfavorable quadrant, keeping only an
absorption terminal, or widening a confirmation window would repair a failed
identity. TPCT therefore uses a dense action-independent state clock.

### SOMA allocation fracture — reserve only

SCAF failed its own source-composition gate and remains retired. A new SOMA
candidate would need a different economic object and cannot delay the selected
TPCT work.

### SEC metadata topology — reserve only

The frozen SEC event source remains broad and causal, but issuer identity,
form-family semantics, and live adapter complexity are less direct than the
already normalized OFR single-segment panel.
