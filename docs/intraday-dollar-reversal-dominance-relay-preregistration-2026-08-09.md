# IDRDR-12 preregistration

IDRDR-12 is frozen before exact incidence, Gross9 clocks, BTC execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. It builds separate six-pair
canonical-dollar factors for the completed 13:00–17:00 and 17:00–21:00 UTC
weekday sub-sessions. The two separately standardized factors must reverse
strict sign and the late factor must equal or exceed the early factor in
absolute magnitude. Completed 24-hour BTC variation must rank in its causal
upper 35%.

The policy enters at 21:05 UTC opposite the late dollar factor and holds twelve
elapsed hours at fixed 0.5 gross. This temporal factor reversal is not DFSR's
full-session factor tail or HVDBR's sign breadth, and no prior event set or
diagnostic control is reused.

RV20 q90 remains a post-stage audit only. Source support, Gross9 novelty, and
strict sequential economics stop at first failure with no session, factor,
reversal, dominance, side, hold, clock, subset, or control repair.
