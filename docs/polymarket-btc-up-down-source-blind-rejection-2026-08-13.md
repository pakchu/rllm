# Polymarket BTC Up/Down source-blind rejection — 2026-08-13

## Verdict

Reject the Polymarket BTC Up/Down axis before preregistration. It is not an
authorized candidate and may not be repaired or reopened in this research
round.

## Why it was considered

The repository contains a terminal Kalshi S&P 500 range-repricing candidate
but no Polymarket candidate. Polymarket's public documentation describes:

- unauthenticated Gamma market discovery, including closed markets;
- unauthenticated CLOB price-history retrieval by asset ID; and
- timestamped historical price observations.

Those interfaces initially made a direct BTC directional prediction-market
observable look operationally independent from exchange flow, funding, open
interest, macro releases, and the Gross9 source families.

Official documentation:

- `https://docs.polymarket.com/quickstart`;
- `https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/fetching-markets`;
- `https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/markets/get-prices-history`; and
- `https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/authentication`.

## Terminal research-boundary failure

Before an exact formula, universe, market-identity grammar, sampling timestamp,
side, volatility gate, decision clock, hold, controls, and stopping rule were
frozen, a public search exposed individual historical BTC Up/Down market pages.
The snippets included example creation/end dates, one-hour resolution rules,
volume fields, and resolved Up/Down outcomes from 2025 and 2026.

That is source incidence and source outcome evidence opened before
preregistration. It violates the required source-blind sequence even though no
BTC execution price, future strategy return, funding row, Gross9 row, PnL,
CAGR, or MDD was inspected.

The exposed examples also showed that the contract family is hourly and
closely tied to the exact Binance BTCUSDT candle it resolves on. Any subsequent
choice of creation-age requirement, quote timestamp, probability-distance
gate, hourly subset, liquidity requirement, or longer BTC hold would now be
post-source selection.

## Stop rule

Do not preregister this family from the exposed evidence. Do not repair the
boundary by changing Polymarket endpoint, contract horizon, sampling offset,
market subset, price threshold, volatility threshold, side, or hold. A future
research program could study prediction-market signals only under a new clean
room whose exact contract grammar and policy are frozen before any historical
market page or API row is opened.
