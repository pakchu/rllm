# TECC-12 preregistration

TECC-12 is frozen before candidate incidence, Gross9 clocks, execution prices,
funding, or post-entry outcomes are opened. At every exact four-hour UTC
boundary it partitions the preceding exact 360 one-minute bars into an early
four-hour and late two-hour block. Both completed returns must have the same
strict sign while quote volume per reported trade expands into its strictly
prior 540-decision top quintile, with at least 360 valid prior observations.
Only the onset of that state enters at decision+5m for twelve elapsed hours at
fixed 0.5 gross.

The candidate measures temporal expansion in average executed ticket under a
directionally sponsored move. It is not cross-asset ticket leadership,
spot/perpetual count share, small-ticket exhaustion, taker imbalance, funding,
or a fitted volatility filter. RV20 q90 remains a later stress audit only.

Source support, Gross9 novelty, and each full-calendar economic stage are
terminal first-failure gates. If every stage passes, the unchanged policy must
have at least eight causal q90 trades, positive q90 return, and positive q90
residual against the same-clock, same-gross forced-long comparator. No rank,
history, onset, side, entry, hold, subset, or control repair is permitted.
