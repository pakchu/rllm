# High-volatility alpha source-exhaustion blocker — 2026-08-13

## Status

The active search is blocked before a new preregistration.  This is not a claim
that no BTC alpha can exist.  It records that, under the unchanged clean-room,
no-repair, causal-history, Gross9-novelty, and strict-economic contracts, the
current environment has no remaining independent observable that can be
evaluated honestly.

The same blocking condition persisted through three consecutive search turns:

1. repository-local source families and apparent unfinished candidates were
   audited;
2. published high-volatility mechanisms and cross-alt dependence families were
   collision-checked; and
3. additional network, SEC, block-transaction, macro-stress, and latest-dated
   candidate paths were audited.

No new source incidence, Gross9 row, execution price, funding value, or
post-entry outcome was opened during these blocker audits.

## Exhausted reproducible families

The repository already contains frozen or terminal candidates covering the
available OHLCV, trade-count, taker-flow, spot/perpetual, premium, funding, FX,
cross-alt, volatility-shape, entropy, jump, tail-dependence, macro-release,
blockspace, Coin Metrics, CFTC, Cboe, SEC, Federal Reserve, Treasury, OFR, and
public-narrative mechanisms reviewed in this continuation.

- confirmation-ladder RBF sponsorship replaced the source coordinate of the

Published conditional-skewness, BTC/equity coskewness, realized-semivariance,
empirical-copula tail dependence, cross-quantilogram, and cross-crypto co-jump

## Remaining independent sources lack admissible history

The strongest genuinely different source axes require an external-state
change:

- complete historical Deribit option surfaces/trades are unavailable from the
  official free API;
- official US spot-BTC ETF primary-market histories are current snapshots or
  non-replayable publications;
- Bitnodes historical node-level snapshots require authenticated PRO or
  separately approved academic archive access;
- historical mempool replacement/backlog state cannot be reconstructed from a
  current node or confirmed transactions;
- CME CF benchmark automation requires an appropriate historical/non-display
  data license; and
- several current-history government/provider exports cannot establish the
  value available at every past decision without a point-in-time vintage
  archive.

These are source-contract failures, not negative economic results.  Shortening
the sample, changing provider after inspecting failures, relaxing completeness,
or promoting a diagnostic control would violate the frozen objective.

## Apparent resumptions are terminal

The resumption audit confirms that HVBLSFX, HVEBPR, HVOFSR, and HVVIXTE cannot
advance.  Their committed artifacts respectively record preregistered history
failure, source-reproduction failure, source-support failure, and
source-support/side-balance failure.  Earlier source-support passes without
matching modern filenames likewise have committed Stage-1 rejection or
postmortem evidence.

## Exact unblock conditions

Work can resume without weakening the objective when at least one of the
following external conditions becomes true:

1. authenticated, legally usable, point-in-time historical access is provided
   for a genuinely independent source such as complete option surfaces,
   Bitnodes snapshots, or another documented archive covering the full frozen
   window;
2. a forward collector for a new independent source accumulates enough history
   to satisfy the unchanged train/test/eval/final design; or
3. a newly published mechanism uses an observable not already represented in
   the repository and that observable has a complete causal archive.

A material request to relax the no-repair, full-window, source-completeness, or
independence constraints would be a different objective, not an unblock of this
one.
