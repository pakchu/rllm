# HVOILSR-12 preregistration — 2026-08-09

HVOILSR-12 is a singleton, outcome-blind test of lagged leveraged sponsorship.
On each exact UTC day it measures whether completed five-minute OI changes led
the next completed five-minute BTC returns in the prior day's direction, then
requires that directional lead score and realized variation to be causally
high. Entry is fixed at 00:05 UTC, side is the prior-day return sign, and hold
is twelve elapsed hours at 0.5 gross.

Source incidence, Gross9 rows, post-entry prices, funding cash, PnL, and RV20
are unopened. The lag, source grid, ranks, thresholds, side, clock, hold, and
controls are frozen; first failure is terminal and controls cannot be promoted.
