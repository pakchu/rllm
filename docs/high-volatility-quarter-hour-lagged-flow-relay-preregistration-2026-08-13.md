# HVQHLF-4 preregistration — 2026-08-13

`HVQHLF-4` tests whether the causally predictable component of BTC quarter-hour
opening flow relays for four hours during elevated realized variation. The signal
uses only the twelve preceding quarter-hour opening imbalances. It does not read
the current boundary imbalance or any post-entry return.

The external basis is Kim and Hansen (2026), *The Quarter-Hour Effect: Periodic
Algorithmic Trading and Return Predictability in Cryptocurrency Futures*
([arXiv 2607.09426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09426)). Their decomposition uses
twelve own lags of quarter-hour opening imbalance; the reported lagged-flow
component has the largest mean contribution at four hours. This experiment makes
three frozen adaptations: one-minute candle flow, strictly-prior rolling OLS, and
high-variation/extreme-strength onset sparsification.

The machine-readable contract is generated by
`training/preregister_high_volatility_quarter_hour_lagged_flow_relay.py`. Source
incidence, Gross9 rows, execution prices, returns, and PnL remain unopened at this
gate. The first failed source, novelty, or sequential economic gate is terminal;
diagnostic controls cannot be promoted.
